- **OPEN BUG in live v4/Original (found by the 5U2D review 2026-10-08, H-1):** Floyd's draw makes slot i range 0..(m_0-1)+i, so in `dealFourteenIdx` seat 0 (the human) draws from 0..45 and can NEVER hold rank 12 (Ace); `dealTenIdx` seat 0 from 0..47 likewise. Not exploitable for money (no stakes) but unfair and visible to anyone reading the contract. - **5U2D v2 — PASS + showdown (2026-10-10, NOT yet deployed):** John's rule: on your claim step you may **Pass** instead of claiming; the opponent draws 2 cards from the SAME deck and each that strictly outranks your same-slot hole card scores them +1 (0/1/2). Both seats may pass in a round → two showdown pairs → **13 cards per round**. Protocol: PASS = `Move.kind 4`, its response slot is `NOOP` (kind 0); moves per round stay 4; the PASS chain link carries `(wins, draw0, draw1)` instead of claim fields. Service: `POST /pass`, `POST /next`; a finished round is HELD (`view.awaitingNext`, `step:'round-end'`, `roundSummary` with both holes + showdowns) until `/next` deals — the next hand never leaks early. UI: Ai score left / You right flanking the board; hole cards nestle between Quit and the Claim/Pass or Accept/Proof-or-Bluff buttons. **Compile lessons (cost ~4 h):** (a) growing the deal to 13 via Floyd + a 48-comparator sorting network did NOT compile — 17 GB RSS + 25 GB swap after 90 min on Terry, OOM at 10 GB on Penelope. (b) The fix is John's "materialise, then exclude": keep the proven 9-card deal and draw each showdown card as "the k-th card not yet on the table" via `skipDealt` (sort the 9 dealt cards, step over each one ≤ the running value; later picks step over earlier picks Floyd-style in index space). Uniform over the remaining 43, pairwise distinct, ~40 compares. (c) **Circuit calls inline their arguments as EXPRESSIONS, not values.** Passing the deal result or a hash digest into another circuit re-materialises it per use. Route every large intermediate through a witness asserted equal to the in-circuit value: `roundDigests()` (3 × `Vector<32,Uint<8>>`, asserted against `dealDigest/shuffleDigest/showdownDigest`), `dealtCards()` (13 indices, asserted against the deal + `showdownCards`), `dealtRanks()` (asserted against `cardToRank(cards[i])`). With all three: **8 min / 11.9 GB on Penelope (`ulimit -v 12500000`), `proveRound` 72,478 rows k=17**; without the digest witness the same contract was still climbing past 11 GB at 17 min on Terry. Referee mirror: `dealNineIndices` + `showdownCards` in `five-up-referee.js`; `finishRound()` now also returns `cards` and `digests`. 33 sim tests + 582 total green against the real circuit.
